Privacy Policy

CallCloser stores your voice-note transcripts, contact names, phone numbers, emails and reminders only on your device. We do not run servers that receive your notes, we have no accounts, and we use no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Speech is converted to text by Apple's Speech framework. When your iPhone and language support on-device recognition, audio never leaves your phone. Otherwise iOS sends the audio of your voice note to Apple's speech servers for transcription, under Apple's privacy policy; CallCloser never receives or stores it. Audio is not saved by CallCloser. Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we receive no payment details.

You can delete all data in Settings › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app. Contact: nickrhigh@yahoo.com.

Optional subscription analytics: if enabled, RevenueCat receives an anonymous install ID, your subscription status and which paywall version you saw. It never receives notes, contacts or transcripts. Last updated: Oct 21, 2026.
