Privacy Policy

FollowUp stores your voice-note transcripts, contact names, phone numbers, emails and reminders only on your device. We do not run servers that receive your notes, we have no accounts, and we use no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Speech is converted to text by Apple's Speech framework, on-device where your iPhone supports it. Audio is not saved by FollowUp. Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we receive no payment details.

You can delete all data in Settings › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app. Contact: nickrhigh@yahoo.com.
